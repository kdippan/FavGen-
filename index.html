What is a Favicon?

A **favicon** (short for "favorite icon") is a small icon associated with a particular website or webpage. It is typically displayed in the browser's address bar, tabs, and bookmarks list.

Originally used just for bookmarks in Internet Explorer, favicons now play a critical role in **Progressive Web Apps (PWA)**, mobile home screens (iOS & Android), and desktop taskbars.
